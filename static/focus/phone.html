Privacy & connection

Events, focus and check-ins are stored on your Pi. Only explicitly requested cloud features leave the device. Provider retention policies may still apply even though API response storage is disabled.

Use trusted Wi-Fi for the local preview. For encrypted access, offline app-shell support and eventual notifications, use a private HTTPS connection. Do not forward the Pi's port to the internet.

Seven-day reviews appear when you next open the app. There are no background push notifications in this build.

In Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. For access while away, a private HTTPS/VPN setup is a separate step.
